# What counts as a short film in v1?

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

How many scenes, what media modality (video clips vs image storyboard), and what playback experience define a successful v1 short film?

## Answer

v1 Short Film = 3–5 Scenes from one Premise. Prefer short video clips per Scene (~4–6s, 16:9, 480p) with on-screen title/dialogue captions; if video fails or user chooses "stills", generate cinematic Image stills and hold each ~5s with a subtle pan. Playback is a sequential Film Player (auto-advance, scrubber by scene). Target total runtime ~15–30s.
